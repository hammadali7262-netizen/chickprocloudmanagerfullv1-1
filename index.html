Software Use Guide

How to run ChickPro every day, module by module.
1
Products.
Set up each saleable item with a unit (KG, BIRD, DOZEN, TRAY, PACK, PIECE, BOX) under Products. This unit follows the item into Purchases, Sales, Inventory and Daily Prices automatically.
2
Purchases.
For Live Chicken, Quantity always means total live weight in KG — enter Bird Count separately. Choose Paid, Partial or Credit; a Partial or Credit purchase shows a Remaining balance and an Add Payment button until it's settled.
3
Chicken Processing.
Record Live KG consumed, Birds Processed, Mortality and Dressed KG produced — yield % is calculated automatically. Then use Distribution to split the dressed output into Chicken Meat Clean, Boneless, Liver & Heart, Gizzard, Neck, Feet, Wings, Skin, Bones or any other category product.
4
Chicken Waste Sales.
Byproducts that aren't regular saleable stock — feathers, blood, condemned birds — get their own entry here so they don't distort your main Sales numbers.
5
Sales.
Record the item, quantity, rate, payment mode, customer name/phone and any remarks. Choose Delivery as the order type to auto-create a delivery task.
6
Daily Prices.
Since cost and sale prices move day to day, log them here each morning. "Save & Apply as Product Default" also updates the product's standing price used elsewhere in the app.
7
Inventory.
Pick a period to see Opening Stock + Purchases + Processing Output + Returns − Sales − Wastage = Closing Stock for every product, plus live bird count.
8
Dashboard & Reports.
Filter by Today, Yesterday, This Week, Last Week, This Month, Last Month or a Custom range to see Sales, Purchases, Expenses, Gross/Net Profit, Orders, AOV, Chicken KG sold, Purchase KG, Processing Yield and Wastage — plus fixed comparisons (Today vs Yesterday, This Week vs Last Week, This Month vs Last Month).
9
Import / Export & Settings.
Export any module to CSV for backup or accounting, and import CSVs for master data. Keep Settings (business name, contacts, address, partner split) up to date — it feeds labels across the app.
Never mix units: live chicken purchases use KG for quantity and birds as a separate count.
