ChickPro Use Guide

Daily sequence

  1. Set Daily Rates.
  2. Enter Purchases. For live chicken, Quantity is total KG and Bird Count is the number of chickens.
  3. Process birds and record Live KG, Birds, Mortality and Dressed KG.
  4. Distribute dressed output into Chicken Meat Clean, Boneless, Liver & Heart, Gizzard, Neck, Feet, Wings, Skin, Bones, etc.
  5. Enter Sales. The register records Date, Item, Qty, Unit, Rate, Total, Payment Mode, Customer Name, Phone and Remarks.
  6. Enter Wastage separately. This is the screen for chicken waste entries that previously failed.
  7. Record Waste Sales separately from regular sales.
  8. Complete Delivery and Day Close at the end of the day.
  9. Review Dashboard / Reports for Today, Week, Month or Custom performance.
Example purchase: 100 birds weighing 185 KG → Quantity = 185 KG; Bird Count = 100.
