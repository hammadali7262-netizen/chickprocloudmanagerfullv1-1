Use Guide

How to run ChickPro every day.

Purchase

For Live Chicken, Quantity means total live KG. Bird Count is the number of birds. Example: 100 birds weighing 185 KG → Quantity 185 KG, Bird Count 100.

Processing

Enter live KG, birds processed, mortality and dressed KG. Yield is calculated automatically.

Distribution

Distribute output into Chicken Meat Clean, Boneless, Liver & Heart, Gizzard, Neck, Feet, Wings, Skin, Bones and other saleable categories.

Sales

Record Walk-in, Phone, WhatsApp, Delivery or B2B sales. Always select the correct unit.

Inventory

Review balances from the cloud inventory ledger.

Closing

Before closing, check sales, purchases, expenses, cash and stock.

Never mix units: live chicken purchases use KG for quantity and birds as a separate count.
